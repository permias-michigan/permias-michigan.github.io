A piece of home, and an open door to Indonesia.

PERMIAS stands for Persatuan Mahasiswa Indonesia Seluruh Amerika Serikat, the name for Indonesian student associations across the United States. Our chapter is also part of the Southeast Asian Network at U-M.

  • Feel at home

    Thanksgiving dinner, a factory outlet trip, and graduation night help students settle in.

  • Celebrate together

    We mark Batik Day, Indonesian Independence Day, and Chinese New Year as a community.

  • Share our culture

    Dance, music, theater, and food bring Indonesia to the wider U-M community.
