About PERMIAS Michigan

We support Indonesian students and families and share Indonesian culture with campus. PERMIAS stands for Persatuan Mahasiswa Indonesia Seluruh Amerika Serikat, the name for Indonesian student associations across the United States. Our chapter is also part of the Southeast Asian Network at U-M.
