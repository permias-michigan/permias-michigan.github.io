About us

We help Indonesian students feel at home in Michigan, and we invite everyone else to experience Indonesia with us.

PERMIAS stands for Persatuan Mahasiswa Indonesia Seluruh Amerika Serikat, the name for Indonesian student associations across the United States. Our chapter is also part of the Southeast Asian Network, which connects Southeast Asian student organizations at U-M.
